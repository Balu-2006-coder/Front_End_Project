COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Everything guests usually want to know before their first visit.

RESERVATIONS

Booking questions

Do you take walk-ins? +

A limited number of bar and lounge seats are kept for walk-ins nightly.

How far ahead should I book? +

For live music nights and weekends, we recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead.

What's your cancellation policy? +

Please cancel at least 4 hours ahead so we can reseat the table.

PRIVATE EVENTS

Private dining questions

How many can the private room hold? +

Up to 40 guests seated, more for standing receptions.

Is there a room minimum? +

Yes, minimums vary by night and season — ask our events team for current pricing.

Can you accommodate AV needs? +

Yes, the room includes a sound system and screen for presentations.

MENU & DIETARY

Food questions

Do you accommodate allergies? +

Yes — note allergies when booking and our kitchen will guide you at the table.

Is there a vegetarian option? +

Several starters and one seasonal main are vegetarian; ask your server for the current option.

Do you offer a kids' menu? +

A simplified kids' menu is available for dinner service.

VISITING

Parking & accessibility

Is there parking? +

Street parking after 6pm, plus a validated lot behind the building.

Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible? +

Yes, the main dining room and private room are both step-free.

Do you allow pets? +

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on our outdoor patio seating.
