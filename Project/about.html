OUR STORY

Two cuisines, one table

Magic Spoon began as a question: what happens when a French kitchen and an Asian pantry share a stove?

THE BEGINNING

Founded on curiosity

Magic Spoon opened its doors in downtown Bellevue with a simple idea: classic French technique, seasoned with the pantry of East and Southeast Asia.

What started as a supper club has grown into one of the neighborhood's most talked-about tables.

THE KITCHEN

Meet the chef

Our executive chef trained in classical French kitchens before spending years cooking across Vietnam, Japan, and Thailand.

Every dish on the menu carries a little of both worlds — never fusion for its own sake.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our philosophy

Technique first

Classic French methods — stocks, sauces, butchery — form the backbone of every dish.

Seasoned by the world

Asian pantry staples bring brightness, heat, and umami to familiar forms.

Hospitality, not performance

We want the room to feel like a great night out, not a show.

SOURCING
